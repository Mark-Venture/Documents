# How-to documents for DroidLink

In addition to the instructions below, I have created various  [Youtube videos](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLwAqmqR0pHhdbFvmWNJg4zcdA0ebzSFpO&si=YSBlItCzzZEpdNff) about DroidLink, its components, connections, wiring, etc. 

* [Electronics_Needed_for_R2.md](https://github.com/Mark-Venture/Documents/blob/main/Electronics_Needed_for_R2.md) - A list of the electronics I have purchased, or have been recomended when building a 3d Printed Mr. Baddeley R2D2 MK4 using Droidlink - It includes parts, links, and comments
* [PoorMans Magic Panel](https://github.com/Mark-Venture/Documents/tree/main/PoorMans_Magic_Panel)  - a simple and cheap Magic Panel led setup for your Astromech.  While not as nice as the one from Printed-Droid.com, or West_Worx,  its very cheap to make and gives you many of the patters and color options.    
* [PWM to SBUS-A Translator](https://github.com/Mark-Venture/Documents/blob/main/PWM_to_SBUSA_translator/ReadMe.MD) -  If you have an ESP32 and breakout board, and can't get your hands on a HotRC SBUS-A receiver,  this is a temporary work-around until you get a real SBUS-A receiver.
* [How to create sequences in DroidLink](https://github.com/Mark-Venture/Documents/blob/main/DroidLink_Sequences_How-To.md) - For DroidLink v1 firmware,  This walks you through creating a sequence of lights, sounds and servo movements in DroidLink
*  _OBSOLETE WITH DROIDLINK V2.x FIRMWARE_  [Sharing Maestro Sequences](https://github.com/Mark-Venture/Documents/blob/main/INPROGRESS_-_Sharing_Maestro_Sequences_-_The_Python_Way.md) - Still "in progress",  A way of exporting and sharing your settings.txt with other users, or adjusting their exported settings for your droid.  This is also helpful if you have to change a servo.
